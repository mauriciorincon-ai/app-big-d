# Design-system extensions from S1 (atlas in the product)

**Status:** accepted · **Date:** 2026-09-29 · **Sprint:** S1 (Atlas de Fabric)

## Context

`design-system.md` (v0.5.2) is read-only for product work: a design change is proposed in the sprint log as a
FORMA look and the design system is extended by ADR (S1 order, «Qué NO tocar»). S1 was the first sprint with real
platform names and the first one where a person used the atlas as a product, so four design decisions were made
outside the mockup. Each was seen by the user before it was built on, and each is recorded in
`sprints/SPRINT_001-implementation-log.md`. This ADR is the extension; the planning house folds it into the next
design-system version.

## Decision

1. **Level-1 block window replaces the «Ficha breve»** (§ 5, «Ficha breve»). Tapping a level-1 block (or a band's
   group without a block) opens the same panel the level-2 card uses. It is titled «Dentro del bloque» / «Inside
   the block» and contains:
   - the block's name and leader line;
   - the block's components drawn by the engine («bloque» view: the level-2 node cards, one below the other, with
     the flows between them; band groups use the compact card);
   - one card per component (`toBlockCards`: type, maturity, leader line, every outgoing and incoming flow with
     its stroke);
   - a link to «Componentes».

   The panel is lateral from 900 px and a modal bottom sheet below that. Focus goes to the title, and Esc or
   «Cerrar» return it to the block. User's FORMA look, 2026-09-29: approved; it was not exactly the window
   rising out of the block they pictured, but they preferred it because it keeps the line of the windows already
   in use. Log: «Mirada de FORMA de la ventana».
2. **Atlas platform selector** (§ 6 and § 10 left it to S1). It is the kit's «Campo»: a label, a native select
   and a drawn chevron, placed in the atlas header.
   - It lists the N platforms ordered by id.
   - A platform without a map reads «— pronto» / «— coming soon» and is disabled.
   - Picking one keeps the language and the reading level.

   The first proposal, a grid of platform cards, was rejected («no es entendible»); the field was approved on
   the second round (stop A, 2026-09-27, look 3).
3. **Trademark note: form A** (D-S1-34). A paragraph at the foot of the legend, generated by the engine with the
   legend it explains (`dg-leyenda-nota`). Nothing new is drawn.
4. **Empty state in context** (A-27, D-S1-41, D-S1-48). A platform without a map has no atlas page; its empty
   state lives on its researcher page: the title «Todavía no hay mapa de …», one line on how a map arrives, and the
   exact `/investigar <id>` command block with «Copiar».

## Consequences

- `design-sync/` shows items 1–3 as generated cards (Componentes · S1 and the legend card).
- The «Ficha breve» row of § 5 is obsolete in the product. The mockup keeps it as the approved reference of its
  time; the fidelity gate compares the map, not that card.
- Next design-system version (planning house): replace the «Ficha breve» row with «Ventana de un bloque», add
  «Selector de plataforma del atlas» to § 5, close the two § 10 debts (selector and trademark note; empty state
  in context), and add the window to the movement table (no motion: it opens and closes by attribute).
